# PassportKit privacy policy draft

PassportKit processes photos on your device. The current build has no internet permission, ads, purchases or analytics. It does not upload photos or use face detection to recognize your identity. The bundled face detector checks composition locally.

You can choose a JPEG with the Android file picker or take a picture. Camera permission lets you use guided capture or the device camera app. Temporary camera files are stored in the app cache and are removed when the capture result is processed. On Android 8 and 9, PassportKit asks for storage access when you save a JPEG to the gallery. On newer Android versions, it uses the system media store.

JPEGs you save remain in Pictures/PassportKit until you delete them. You choose where to save PDFs. A document provider, external camera app or share target you choose handles files according to its own policies. PassportKit does not keep a photo history or back up its app data.

Before publishing this policy, add the operator's name and contact address, confirm the policy against the final signed build, and publish it at a public HTTPS URL.

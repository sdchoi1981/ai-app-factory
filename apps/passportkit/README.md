# PassportKit / 여권사진 만들기
Version 1.0.0. Native Android 8+; Korean and English. Korean passport photo preparation only. Official references verified 2026-11-27: https://www.passport.go.kr/home/kor/contents.do?menuPos=32 and https://www.passport.go.kr/home/kor/contents.do?menuPos=12 . Online JPEG 413×531 and ≤500,000 bytes; 35×45 mm physical print; 32–36 mm crown-to-chin manual guides. No beautification, artificial background replacement or generation. No guarantee of acceptance. Face pose and simple image quality hints are not biometric identity recognition or official validation. Use compliant original photo, manually check background, expression, recency, crown and chin. System camera (not CameraX in this first version), document picker, bundled ML Kit 16.1.7, EXIF orientation, bounded decode, local crop, JPEG and exact-size PDF 4×6/5×7/A4 through user-selected document provider. No app Internet, camera or storage permission; camera app gets temporary URI only; camera cache removed after result; no history/backup/analytics/ads/billing. Selected output provider or camera app may have its own policies. No live ad IDs or payments; future pricing and store setup require authorization. Physical device camera, fold/rotation, EXIF, face model, export, print scaling and privacy manifest checks needed. No Japanese or non-Korean templates yet. Source and output remain on device unless user explicitly chooses an external document provider.

Only original JPEG input is accepted; transparent PNGs and edited-background composites are not supported by the input flow. The app cannot detect whether an externally supplied JPEG was previously manipulated. Permission verification runs separately on the final APK. Device testing remains necessary.

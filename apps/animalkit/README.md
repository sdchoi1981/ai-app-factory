# AnimalKit 2.0.0

Offline illustrated animal facts and family-friendly quizzes.

Twelve animal fact cards and three-choice quizzes. Emoji illustrations, no recordings or photographs in v1. Android 11+, English, Korean and Japanese. No network, accounts, analytics, advertising or billing. Automated build and real-device verification required; release disabled.

Version 2 adds branded rounded controls, a large interactive visual panel and haptics. See DESIGN.md for scope and limitations. CI and device validation pending.

The shared design system provides light and dark cards, accessible status announcements, and edge-to-edge layout. A quiz keeps its answer hidden until a choice is made, reveals the animal afterward, and avoids immediately repeating the previous question. Progress survives activity recreation without adding persistent tracking.
